# S-001: Start a new game and pick a club (evolution record)

Finalized 2026-10-15. Delivery history stays in `docs/feature/S-001/` (feature-delta, slices, `deliver/roadmap.json`, `deliver/execution-log.json`).

## Summary
Sam taps "New game" on the title screen, sees "Choose your club" with 8 generated fictional clubs, taps one and lands on a screen headed by the club name. The league is generated purely from a seed (`?seed=<int>`, falling back to `DEFAULT_SEED` on anything invalid). The chosen club is held in memory only. KPI: title to chosen club in 2 taps, measured by the e2e tap count.

## Key decisions
- ADR-0002 plain-DOM views via `el()`; ADR-0003 functional core / imperative shell (`update`, `Screen`); ADR-0004 seed source and generated world. No new ADR was needed.
- D2: only `main.ts` reads `location.search`; `parseSeed(search)` is pure.
- D3: name uniqueness by construction (towns drawn without replacement).
- D5: chosen club in `GameState`, no persistence. D6: title screen migrated to `el()` with a `dispatch` hook.
- Strategy: recreate deploy on Pages; rollback by `workflow_dispatch` of `deploy.yml` on a known-good ref; mutation nightly-delta (Phase 5 skipped).

4. Smoke does not assert the deployed commit SHA.

## Lessons
- Pure `parseSeed` and `update` made the walking skeleton testable in Node and in the browser with the same expectations.
- Console-error checks in e2e caught an incidental favicon 404; static assets referenced by `index.html` need explicit handling.
- Lean waves with pre-answered gates kept the story to four small steps with a green suite at each commit.
